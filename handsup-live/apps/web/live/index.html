    Delay: from the last frame of each 0.32 s chunk to its result, on this device. Signs are read in chunks of 8 frames at 25 frames a second.

    Research demo. The sign model (fc10, a streaming transformer from a master's thesis) was trained on the RWTH-PHOENIX-Weather 2014 data set, which is for non-commercial research only; so is this model. Hands Up will train its own models on its own data before any product use.

    Everything runs in this browser: MediaPipe finds the body and hand points, and the sign model reads only those points. No video, image or point is stored or sent anywhere, there are no cookies and no analytics, and the camera turns off when you leave the page.

    MediaPipe (Apache-2.0), ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Glosses: PHOENIX-2014 annotation conventions.
